# U2 stones: stone variants, raw metal bricks, sturdy stone and celebratory lamps

Reference: Quark 4.1-488 (`release-4.1-488+1.21.1`), modules `building/MoreStoneVariants`,
`building/RawMetalBricks`, `building/SturdyStone`, `building/CelebratoryLamps`, their block classes
(`SturdyStoneBlock`, `CelebratoryLampBlock`), recipes, loot tables, block tags and `en_us.json`.
Behaviour only; textures are original (`tools/textures/stones.ps1`), pack JSON is generated by
`tools/pack/stones.ps1`.

## 1. Modules covered

### MoreStoneVariants
Adds polished calcite and polished dripstone (with stairs and slabs), stairs/slabs/walls for raw
calcite and dripstone blocks, and for granite, diorite, andesite, calcite and dripstone: **bricks**
(with stairs, slab, wall), **chiseled bricks** and a **pillar**. Vertical slabs of all these come
from Quark's VerticalSlabs (U1's module flag `vertical_slabs`). Quark also runs the same "bricks /
chiseled / pillar" set for its own new stones (limestone, jasper, shale, myalite) when NewStoneTypes
is enabled; those materials belong to U3 (SPEC §2.3 ownership rule), see §7.

Quark 4.1-488 has no tuff, deepslate or blackstone variants here (vanilla 1.20.3+ added tuff
bricks / polished tuff, so Quark dropped them).

### RawMetalBricks
Raw iron, raw gold and raw copper bricks (with stairs, slab, wall; vertical slab via VerticalSlabs),
crafted from raw metal blocks. They can be turned back into raw metal and (UtilityRecipes'
`smeltRawOreBlocks`) smelted into metal blocks at 9x the normal smelting time.

### SturdyStone
Sturdy Stone: a stone block that pistons can neither push nor pull (`PushReaction.BLOCK`), harder than
stone (hardness 4, resistance 10), crafted from 9 cobblestone and uncrafted back.

### CelebratoryLamps
Stone Lamp and Stone Brick Lamp: stone-looking blocks that emit light (`lightLevel`, default 15),
added for 10 years of Vazkii's mods. Advanced tooltips show "Celebrating 10 Years of Vazkii's Mods (2021)".

## 2. Blocks and items

All blocks: creative category Construction, sound `stone` (Quark: `SoundType.CALCITE` /
`DRIPSTONE_BLOCK` for calcite/dripstone variants — Bedrock sound sets `calcite` and
`dripstone_block` are used), require a pickaxe for drops unless noted, drop themselves (slabs 2
when double). Shapes use the generated shape contract (`tools/pack/README.md`): stairs, slabs,
vertical slabs, walls (tags `lepton:stairs`, `lepton:slab`, `lepton:vertical_slab`, `lepton:wall`),
pillars (`lepton:pillar`, `minecraft:block_face` axis).

### 2.1 MoreStoneVariants

Bricks, chiseled bricks and pillars: hardness 1.5, resistance 6 (Quark `strength(1.5, 6)`). Polished
calcite / dripstone and the shapes of calcite / dripstone blocks copy the raw block (calcite 0.75 / 0.75,
dripstone block 1.5 / 1). Sounds: `calcite` / `dripstone_block` for the calcite / dripstone family,
`stone` otherwise.

vertical slab 2, own stairs 1, slab 2, vertical slab 2, wall 1; polished calcite/dripstone -> its

### 2.2 RawMetalBricks

Copies of the raw metal blocks: hardness 5, resistance 6; drops need a stone-tier pickaxe or better
(raw gold bricks: iron tier or better, Quark tags `needs_stone_tool` / `needs_iron_tool`; Quark leaves
the walls out of those tags, so walls drop with any pickaxe).

| Id | English | Shapes |
|---|---|---|
| `lepton:raw_iron_bricks` | Raw Iron Bricks | block, `_stairs` (Raw Iron Brick Stairs), `_slab`, `_wall`, `_vertical_slab` |
| `lepton:raw_gold_bricks` | Raw Gold Bricks | same |
| `lepton:raw_copper_bricks` | Raw Copper Bricks | same |

Recipes: 2x2 raw metal blocks -> 4 bricks; 1 bricks -> 9 raw metal (shapeless); shapes as above
(crafting + stonecutter from the bricks). Furnace / blast furnace: bricks -> iron / gold / copper
block, 6.3 / 9 / 6.3 XP, 1800 / 900 ticks (gated by U8's `smeltRawOreBlocks`).

### 2.3 SturdyStone

`lepton:sturdy_stone` "Sturdy Stone": hardness 4, resistance 10, pickaxe; immovable by pistons
(`minecraft:movable` `immovable`). Recipes: 3x3 cobblestone -> 1; sturdy stone -> 9 cobblestone.
(Quark's alternative recipe when the mod Cyclic is installed is not ported.)

### 2.4 CelebratoryLamps

`lepton:stone_lamp` "Stone Lamp" (copies stone: hardness 1.5, resistance 6) and
`lepton:stone_brick_lamp` "Stone Brick Lamp" (copies stone bricks), light `lightLevel`.
State `lepton:light` (int 0..15, default 15) selects the emitted light. Recipes (3 lamps):

```
SSS     S = stone (stone lamp) / stone bricks (stone brick lamp)
GTG     G = glass, T = torch
SSS
```

## 3. Mechanics

- **Disabled content** (SPEC §2.3): each block/recipe carries a gate. `enableMoreStoneVariants`
  gates everything of 2.1; bricks and their shapes also need `enableBricks`; chiseled bricks need
  `enableBricks && enableChiseledBricks`; pillars need `enablePillar`. Vertical slabs and their
  recipes also need U1's `enableVerticalSlabs` (read from `config/vslabs.json`, true when missing).
  RawMetalBricks / SturdyStone / CelebratoryLamps are gated by their module key. Smelting raw metal
  bricks also needs U8's `enableUtilityRecipes && smeltRawOreBlocks` (`config/storage.json`, true when
  missing). A recipe is gated by its result and every Lepton ingredient. Disabled recipes are not
  loaded (`Recipes::_loadRecipe` hook) and disabled blocks are left out of the creative inventory
  (`CreativeItemInitializer::_addCreativeItemServer` hook); both apply when a world loads (Quark: on
  `/reload` / restart), so `/quark reload` takes effect on the next world load.
- **Sturdy stone** cannot be pushed or pulled by pistons and sticky pistons.
- **Lamp light**: newly placed lamps (any placement path: players, commands, structures) are set to
  `lepton:light = lightLevel` (clamped 0..15) one tick after the block change, so a changed
  `lightLevel` applies to every lamp placed afterwards (Quark: after a restart).
- **Raw metal bricks smelting**: a furnace or blast furnace smelting raw metal bricks advances only
  every 9th tick (1800 / 900 ticks per item) and yields 6.3 XP (gold 9) per item.
- **Pillars** take the axis of the clicked face (generated `lepton:pillar` behaviour).

## 7. Bedrock differences / not ported

- **New stones' variants** (limestone, jasper, shale, myalite bricks / chiseled / pillar): owned by U3
  (material owner). Quark gates them by MoreStoneVariants' `enableBricks` / `enableChiseledBricks` /
  `enablePillar`; U3 can read those keys from `config/stones.json`.
- **Vertical slabs of vanilla granite/diorite/andesite (+ polished)** belong to U1 (vanilla materials).
- **Creative order**: Bedrock creative groups replace Quark's "next to X" placement: polished blocks
  in `itemGroup.name.stone`, bricks/chiseled/pillars/raw bricks in `itemGroup.name.stoneBrick`, shapes
  in the stairs/slab/walls groups.
- **Advanced tooltips**: Bedrock has no advanced-tooltip mode (F3+H); the client mod shows the
  celebration line on lamp tooltips always. Vanilla clients don't see it.
- **Lamp light level** is a block state: lamps placed before a `lightLevel` change keep their light.
- **Cooking time / XP**: Bedrock recipe JSON has neither; done natively in the furnace tick and XP hooks.
- Quark's Cyclic-compat recipe, advancements and `@Hint` (JEI) are not ported (SPEC §8).

# U5 palette: decorative building palettes (shingles, framed glass, metal, paper, thatch, grates)

Behaviour reference: Quark 4.1-488 (`release-4.1-488+1.21.1`), modules `ShinglesModule`,
`FramedGlassModule`, `GoldBarsModule`, `IndustrialPaletteModule`, `JapanesePaletteModule`,
`NetherBrickFenceGateModule`, `ThatchModule`, `GrateModule` and their blocks (`GrateBlock`,
`ThatchBlock`, `PaperLanternBlock`, `PaperWallBlock`, `BambooMatBlock`, `BambooMatCarpetBlock`,
`VariantLadderBlock`), data (recipes, loot tables, tags) and `en_us.json`.

Everything here is generated by `tools/pack/palette.ps1` (block/item/recipe/loot JSON, fragments,
`src/feature/palette/PaletteIds.inc`) and `tools/textures/palette.ps1` (textures), and driven
natively by `src/feature/palette/Palette.cpp`.

## 1. Modules covered

### Shingles
Terracotta Shingles: a full block, stairs, slab and vertical slab for plain terracotta and for each
of the 16 dyed terracottas (17 sets, 68 blocks). Properties copied from the parent terracotta.

### FramedGlass
Framed Glass (iron-framed glass that, unlike vanilla glass, drops itself), its pane, and 16 dyed
("stained") framed glass blocks with their panes (34 blocks). Strength 3 / resistance 10, glass
sound, mined with a pickaxe.

### GoldBars
Gold Bars: an iron-bars-like pane block made of gold. With `generateInNetherFortress` (default on)
every Nether Brick Fence that a Nether Fortress places during world generation becomes Gold Bars
(keeping its connections).

### IndustrialPalette
Iron Plate and Rusty Iron Plate (each with stairs, slab, vertical slab), Iron Pillar (axis block)
and Iron Ladder (a metal ladder). Iron block properties (strength 5 / 6, metal sound, pickaxe).
Options `enableIronPlates` (plates, rusty plates, their shapes and the pillar) and
`enableIronLadder`. "Rusting" in Quark is only the crafting recipe (iron + water bucket); Quark
4.1-488 has no in-world weathering of plates, so neither does Lepton.

### JapanesePalette
Paper Lantern and Decorated Paper Lantern (light 15, lantern-on-a-post model), Paper Wall, Big Paper
Wall and Decorated Paper Wall (pane-like walls), Bamboo Mat Block and Bamboo Mat (a 1/16 carpet).
Options `enablePaperBlocks` (lanterns and walls) and `enableBambooMats`.

### NetherBrickFenceGate
A fence gate made of nether bricks (strength 2 / 6, pickaxe, nether brick sound) that opens like
wooden gates and connects to Nether Brick Fences.

### Thatch
Thatch (+ stairs, slab, vertical slab): a hay-like, very flammable block. Falling onto a Thatch
block multiplies fall damage by `fallDamageMultiplier` (0.5). Thatch is compostable (65 %).

### Grate
Iron Grate: a 1/16 thick metal floor at the top of its block. Items and experience orbs fall
through it; animals (and water animals) that are not on a lead cannot step onto it; mobs cannot
spawn on it; it can be waterlogged.

## 3. Mechanics

- **Disabled content** (`enable<Module>`, `enableIronPlates`, `enableIronLadder`,
  `enablePaperBlocks`, `enableBambooMats` false): the unit's recipes producing that content are
  refused by the server (hook on `Recipes::getRecipeByNetId`, so crafting and stonecutting fail),
  and placing such a block is cancelled. Read live (works after `/quark reload`).
- **Gold bars in fortresses** (`enableGoldBars && generateInNetherFortress`): hook on
  `StructurePiece::placeBlock`; when a Nether Fortress piece places `minecraft:nether_brick_fence`,
  `lepton:gold_bars` is placed instead. The position is queued; on the server tick (once the chunk
  is loaded) the pane connections are recomputed (`shapes::updateShape`). Queued positions are
  saved in the world DB (`lp:palette:bars`) so they survive restarts.
- **Thatch fall cushioning** (`enableThatch`, `fallDamageMultiplier` 0..1, default 0.5): hook on
  `BlockEntityFallOnEvent::handleActorFallDamage`; when the block fallen on carries
  `lepton:thatch`, the damage multiplier is multiplied by `fallDamageMultiplier` (Java
  `causeFallDamage(distance, multiplier)`).
- **Thatch composting**: using thatch on a composter (level < 7) consumes one thatch (not in
  creative) and raises the level with 65 % chance; level 7 turns to ready (8) after 20 ticks, as
  vanilla does.
- **Grate** (`enableGrate`):
  - Collision (hooks on `BlockType::getCollisionShape` / `addCollisionShapes` for the grate's
    block type): item entities and experience orbs get no collision (they fall through);
    animals (`ActorType` family Animal) and water animals that are not leashed and are not
    standing on a grate get a box from y 15/16 to `17/16 + maxAutoStep`, so they cannot step onto
    it (Java uses the step-height attribute; Bedrock's max auto step is the equivalent); everything
    else gets the 1/16 top plate.
  - No mob spawns on grates (hook `BlockType::canSpawnOn`).
  - Waterloggable through `minecraft:liquid_detection` (water only).
- **Iron ladder** (`enableIronLadder`): placed on the side of a block (`minecraft:block_face`
  trait; the ladder hangs on the clicked block). Breaks (drops itself) when the block it hangs on
  is no longer a full solid block (checked on `BlockChangedEvent`, deferred one tick). Blocks
  tagged `lepton:climbable` get the engine's `Climbable` block property at install, so entities
  climb them exactly like vanilla ladders on the server and on mod clients.
- **Bamboo mats** (`enableBambooMats`): Java placement: facing = the player's horizontal look
  direction, or `down` when the pitch is > 70°; when the block behind (opposite the facing) is not
  a mat of the same kind facing that way but the block in front is a mat facing the same way, the
  facing is reversed. Applied one tick after placement. The carpet breaks (drops) when the block
  below becomes air.
- **Paper lanterns**: light 15; walls light 0.

## 6. Strings

No mod text (no chat messages, tooltips or screens). Block names (RP `texts.d`) are Quark's English
names listed in §2; zh_CN follows Quark's `zh_cn.json`. Quark's only other string is the JEI hint
`quark.jei.hint.grate` ("Animals that aren't leashed will not walk on this, and items will fall
through."), kept in `assets/lang/*/palette.json` for completeness (JEI does not exist on Bedrock).

## 7. Bedrock differences / not ported

- **Lava-logged grates**: Bedrock custom blocks can only contain water (`minecraft:liquid_detection`
  supports water only), so grates cannot hold lava (Quark: lava-logged grates glow at light 15 and
  turn into obsidian next to water). Not possible.
- **Crawl-space / `isLog` integrations** of `GrateBlock` (Zeta interfaces for other Java mods): no
  meaning on Bedrock.
- **Disabled content in the creative inventory**: Bedrock sends the creative inventory once from
  the behaviour pack; disabled blocks stay listed there (but cannot be crafted or placed).
- **Fence gate sounds**: the nether brick gate plays Bedrock's generic fence gate open/close sound
  (played by `game/Shapes`), not the nether wood one.
- **Iron ladder sounds**: metal break/place/hit (Quark: metal break/place/hit + ladder step/fall).
- **Climbing for vanilla clients**: the `Climbable` property is set on the server and in the mod
  client; a vanilla client on a BDS server predicts no climbing and is corrected by the server's
  authoritative movement (climbing works but may stutter).
- **Flammability**: Bedrock's `minecraft:flammable` maps Java's spread speed to
  `catch_chance_modifier` and flammability to `destroy_chance_modifier`. Thatch has no spread speed
  in Quark (0), which on Bedrock would make it never catch: it uses 5 (like planks) so it can burn.
- **Bamboo mats and lava**: Quark marks them `ignitedByLava`; Bedrock has no separate lava-ignition
  flag for custom blocks: they are not flammable.
- **Advancement unlocks / recipe conditions**: Bedrock unlocks recipes from the `unlock` items;
  conditions are replaced by the native recipe block (§3).

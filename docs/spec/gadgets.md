# U23 gadgets: abacus, pathfinder maps, ambience discs, Endermosh, compasses everywhere, tiny potato

Quark 4.1-488 (tag `release-4.1-488+1.21.1`) modules `tools/Abacus`, `tools/PathfinderMaps`,
`tools/AmbientDiscs`, `tools/EndermoshMusicDisc`, `tweaks/CompassesWorkEverywhere` and
`oddities/TinyPotato`. Behaviour only: no code, texture, model or sound comes from Quark. Textures are
drawn by `tools/textures/gadgets.ps1`, sounds are generated by `tools/pack/gadgets_audio.py`
(original synthesis, see §2.3) and packed by `tools/pack/gadgets.ps1`.

## 1. Modules covered

### Abacus
An item. Using it on a block binds that block; using it again (on any block) unbinds it. While the
abacus is held (main or off hand) and the player looks at a non-air block, it shows the Manhattan
distance between the looked-at block and the bound block, **+1** (it counts blocks, not gaps), up to
48 (`48+` beyond). Quark draws the abacus icon and the number right of the crosshair, and outlines
the bound block plus the three axis-aligned legs from it to the looked-at block in
`highlightColor`.

### PathfinderMaps
The Pathfinder's Quill holds a target biome. Using it starts a search (only one searching quill per
player). The search walks a square spiral around the position where it started, 32 blocks per
step, testing the biome at several heights per column, `pathfindersQuillSpeed` steps per tick (or
on a worker thread when `multiThreaded`). When the biome is found the quill turns into a filled
map (scale 2, centred on the target) with a biome preview and an X marker, named
"<Biome> Pathfinder Map". If the spiral reaches `searchRadius` without finding it, the quill
resets (`allowRetrying`) or turns into nothing. Quills are sold by Cartographers (one trade per
enabled biome, at its villager level, emerald price in [min, max] plus a compass) and by Wandering
Traders. A HUD line "Searching..." with a bobbing quill shows while a quill searches.

### AmbientDiscs
Eight "Ambience Disc" records (drips, ocean, rain, wind, fire, clock, crickets, chatter) that loop
forever in a jukebox at volume `volume`. When a skeleton (or stray/bogged: any skeleton-type mob
whose killer entity is a skeleton) kills a spider or cave spider, one random ambience disc drops.

### EndermoshMusicDisc
The record "Kain Vinosec - Endermosh" (comparator 14, 189.4 s), found in End City treasure chests
(weight 5, quality 1). Optionally (`playEndermoshDuringEnderdragonFight`, off by default) it plays
as music during the Ender Dragon fight.

### CompassesWorkEverywhere
Compasses point to the portal the player came through in the Nether (once the player stood in a
nether portal block in the Nether with the compass), and to the world origin (exit portal) in the
End. With the nerfs on, a compass or clock that never ticked in a player's inventory shows a fixed
frame (no recipe-book cheese).

### TinyPotato
A tiny potato block (4x6x4 px) that believes in you. Using it makes it jump and puts out a heart;
it holds one item per side (6 slots) that the player puts in / takes out with the hand. Names
(anvil) change its look (name variants), give a few names a sound, and potatoes holding potatoes
tell you not to talk to them or their son ever again. Randomly jumps (1/100 per tick).

### 2.3 Textures and sounds

Original pixel art from `tools/textures/gadgets.ps1`: abacus, quill (blank and inscribed), 9 disc

## 3. Mechanics

### Abacus
- Binding: `PlayerInteractBlockEvent` with `lepton:abacus` in the main hand. Bound -> unbind;
  unbound -> bind the clicked block. Stored in the stack's user data `boundPosX/Y/Z` (Y = -999
  means unbound, as Quark). Debounced 5 ticks (Bedrock repeats the use while held).
- Count: `clamp(|dx|+|dy|+|dz|, 0, 48)` between the looked-at block (server ray trace from the eyes,
  survival reach 5.0 / creative 6.0... Bedrock reach: 5.5 survival, 12 creative is client-side; we
  use 5.0 survival and 6.0 creative, Java's `blockInteractionRange` 4.5 + 0.5/1.5) and the bound
  block; nothing is shown when the looked-at block is air or nothing is bound. Shown as `n+1`
  (`48+` when the clamped value is 48). Checked every 2 ticks for players holding an abacus in
  either hand.
- Mod clients: the server sends `gadgets:abacus` (count); the client draws the abacus icon at
  (w/2+10, h/2-7) and the text at +17,+5 (white, shadow).
- Outline (every client): the bound block and the three legs (x, then y, then z) are drawn as thin
  lines in `highlightColor` with the `lepton:abacus_highlight` particle (one camera-facing quad per
  box edge, sent only to the holder, refreshed every 10 ticks).
- Vanilla clients: the action bar shows the number (`48+`).

### PathfinderMaps
- Quill user data: `targetBiome` (Java id, e.g. `minecraft:jungle`), `biomeColor` (ARGB int),
  `isSearching`, `sourceX/Z`, `posX/Z`, `posLeg`, `posLegIndex`. Lore lines show the biome name
  (and "Searching..." in blue while searching) for every client; mod clients also get the same
  tooltip lines natively (blank quills: "Blank").
- Use (`PlayerUseItemEvent`): a blank quill does nothing. If another quill in the inventory is
  already searching: action bar "A Pathfinder's Quill is already searching..." and nothing else.
  Otherwise plays `item.book.page_turn` (0.5 volume) and starts searching from the player's block
  x/z.
- Search (every tick, the first searching quill of the inventory: main, then off hand, then armor):
  the spiral of Quark (`step = 32`, directions east, south, west, north, leg size `leg/2 + 1`,
  `maxLegs = 4 * floor(searchRadius/32)`), at each column the heights `y, y+64, y-64, y+128, ...`
  within the dimension (Java `Mth.outFromOrigin(y, min+1, max, 64)`), biome from the dimension's
  biome source (`WorldGenerator::getBiomeSource().getBiomeArea`), no chunk generation needed.
  `pathfindersQuillSpeed` columns per tick, or, with `multiThreaded`, the whole search on a worker
  thread (results cached per start position and biome, as Quark).
- Success: the quill becomes a `minecraft:filled_map` (scale 2, centred on the target), with the
  biome preview rendered, a red X decoration at the target and the custom name
  "<Biome> Pathfinder Map". Fail: with `allowRetrying` the quill is reset (keeps its biome) and the
  action bar says "... Try somewhere else."; without, the quill is consumed with "... could not
  find any biome nearby.". Both play `note.chime` (0.5).
- Biome ids: config and items use Java ids; the Bedrock biome name is looked up in a table
  (`snowy_plains -> ice_plains`, `windswept_hills -> extreme_hills`, `dark_forest -> roofed_forest`,
  `swamp -> swampland`, `old_growth_pine_taiga -> mega_taiga`, `badlands -> mesa`,
  `mushroom_fields -> mushroom_island`, `ice_spikes -> ice_plains_spikes`, others unchanged). Ids
  that are not in the table are used as Bedrock names directly (custom maps may use either).
- Built-in trades (Java level / price / colour): snowy_plains, windswept_hills, dark_forest, desert,
  savanna (4 / 8-14), swamp, mangrove_swamp, old_growth_pine_taiga (4 / 12-18), flower_forest,
  jungle, bamboo_jungle, badlands (5 / 16-22), mushroom_fields, ice_spikes, cherry_grove
  (5 / 20-26). Custom lines `<id>,<level>,<min_price>,<max_price>,<color>[,<name>]` (5 or 6 tokens,
  colour ARGB hex) are appended; bad lines are logged and skipped.
- Cartographer (`addToCartographer`): after a cartographer generates its trades
  (`EconomyTradeableComponent::_generateTrades`), one offer per enabled trade is added at tier
  `level - 1`: `price` emeralds (random in [min, max]) + 1 compass -> quill, 12 uses, villager xp
  `xpFromTrade * max(1, level - 1)`, price multiplier 0.2.
- Wandering trader: `addToWanderingTraderForced` adds one random quill offer (emeralds only) at the
  front of a new trader's list once; otherwise `addToWanderingTraderGeneric`/`Rare` add one random
  offer to the generated list.
- Creative inventory: one quill per trade can't be listed (Bedrock creative items have no data);
  `/quark gadgets quill <biome>` (op) gives an inscribed quill instead. The `quark:pathfinders_quill`
  loot function becomes the same command path.
- The "Road to Tomorrow" advancement trigger is not ported (no custom advancements, SPEC §8).
- HUD (mod clients, `drawHud`): "Searching" + 0-3 dots (every 10 ticks) at x 5, y `20` (`hudOnTop`)
  or `height - 15`, white with shadow; the quill icon bobs 15 px above (x `+ (sin(t)+1)*20`,
  y `+ sin(8t) - 1` when `cos(t) >= 0`, t = ticks * 0.1). The server tells mod clients which item is
  searching with `gadgets:quill` (searching on/off).

### AmbientDiscs
- `MobDieEvent`: if `dropOnSpiderKill`, the dead mob is `minecraft:spider`/`minecraft:cave_spider`
  and the killer entity is `minecraft:skeleton`/`stray`/`bogged`/`wither_skeleton`
  (Java `instanceof Skeleton` = skeleton only; Bedrock's projectile owner is the killer), drop one
  random ambience disc (with its description as lore).
- Jukebox: Bedrock record items only accept vanilla sound events, so the discs are `minecraft:record`
  items declaring `record.13`; Lepton skips the jukebox's own playback for them
  (`JukeboxBlockActor::_startRecordSound`) and plays `lepton.ambient.<name>` itself, again every 24 s
  (the loop length) while the disc stays inside and the chunk is loaded, at volume `volume` (heard
  within `volume * 16` blocks, like Java's linear 48 at volume 3). Jukeboxes are found through
  `setRecord` / `load`. Taking the disc out stops the sound (`StopSoundPacket`). Endermosh is played
  the same way (`lepton.music.endermosh`, once, 64 blocks).

### EndermoshMusicDisc
- End city loot (`addToEndCityLoot`): when an End City treasure chest fills its loot
  (`LootTable` fill hook, table `loot_tables/chests/end_city_treasure.json`), each of the vanilla
  pool's rolls has the chance `lootWeight / (W + lootWeight)` (W = the pool's total weight, read
  from the table at runtime) to be replaced by the disc; `lootQuality` adds `quality * luck` to the
  weight (luck is 0 for chests, so it only matters through `/loot ... luck`).
- Dragon fight music (`playEndermoshDuringEnderdragonFight`, off by default): the server checks
  every second for players in the End within 54.7 blocks of x/z 0 (`x²+z² < 3000`) while the ender
  dragon is alive; after 50 such ticks it plays `lepton.music.endermosh_fight` for them and shows
  "Now playing: Music Disc - Kain Vinosec - Endermosh" (action bar); it stops when the fight ends.

### CompassesWorkEverywhere
- Server, every tick, every `minecraft:compass` and `minecraft:clock` in a player's inventory:
  - clock: sets `quark:clock_calculated`.
  - compass: first tick sets `quark:compass_calculated` and `quark:was_in_nether` (in the Nether?).
    Later: in the Nether, not marked, standing in a `minecraft:portal` block -> store
    `quark:nether_x/z` (that portal), `was_in_nether` and `pos_set`; out of the Nether after having
    been in it -> clear `was_in_nether`, `pos_set`.
  The inventory is re-sent only when a stack changed.
- Client mod (hook `AbstractCompassItem::getAnimationFrameFor`, `ClockItem::getAnimationFrameFor`):
  - compass nerf (`enableCompassNerf`): uncalculated compass -> frame 0;
  - End (`enableEnd`): point to block 0,0,0; Nether (`enableNether`): point to the stored portal when
    `pos_set`, else spin; elsewhere vanilla.
  - clock nerf (`enableClockNerf`): uncalculated clock -> frame 0.
  Lodestone and recovery compasses are separate items on Bedrock and keep vanilla behaviour, as in
  Quark (a lodestone-bound compass is untouched).

### TinyPotato
- Per-block data in the world DB `lp:gadgets:tater:<dim>:<x>:<y>:<z>`: `name`, `angry`, `items[6]`
  (SNBT), plus the list of known potatoes `lp:gadgets:taters` (for random jumps and the Halloween
  look).
- Placement: the placed item's custom name and angry flag are copied to the block (next tick), and
  the variant state is set.
- Use (any item or empty hand, `PlayerInteractBlockEvent`, cancelled, applied next tick), slot =
  clicked face (`down 0, up 1, north 2, south 3, west 4, east 5`):
  - slot full and hand empty: the slot's item goes to the hand;
  - hand not empty: one item moves into the slot; if the hand is now empty it receives the slot's
    old item, otherwise the old item goes back into the inventory (or drops).
  - then: jump (when not jumping: 20 ticks, `lepton:jump` frames), heart particle at a random spot
    on top, special name sounds with a 20 tick cooldown: "shia labeouf" -> do_it, "joe biden" ->
    soda, "yungnickyoung" -> yung; if it holds tiny potatoes: action bar "Don't talk to me or my
    son ever again." (children if more than one potato; the one potato's name gives
    daughter/child/children/tomato as Quark's table).
- Random jump: 1 in 100 per tick for every loaded potato.
- Jump frames: `t` = 20..1 ticks left -> frame `ceil(t/2)`; frame k shows the potato raised
  `|sin(k*2/10*pi)| * 0.2` blocks, tilted `sin(...) * 2` degrees and shifted `sin(...) * 0.05` (the
  geometry of every frame is generated).
- Item: names with a typo of "vazkii" (`vaskii, vazki, voskii, vazkkii, vazkki, vazzki, vaskki,
  vozkii, vazkil, vaskil, vazkill, vaskill, vaski`) in a player's inventory send the next line of
  the "wrong neighbourhood" song (17 lines, red) every 30 ticks, counted in the stack's user data.
- Mod clients see the potato's name under it when they look at it (HUD label; "[WIP]" under
  "pahimar", "(soon)" under "soaryn"); vanilla clients get the name in the action bar.

## 7. Bedrock differences / not ported

- **Abacus item model**: Quark switches the item model with the count (`abacus_0..48`). Bedrock
  items have one icon per item; the number is shown on the HUD / action bar instead.
- **Quill/map tint**: Quark tints the inscribed quill and the map item with the biome colour.
  Bedrock has no per-stack item tint; the colour is stored on the quill (and used for the HUD
  quill) but icons are not tinted.
- **Creative tab quills** and the `quark:pathfinders_quill` loot function: Bedrock creative items
  can't carry data; replaced by `/quark gadgets quill <biome>`.
- **Advancements** (`pat_potato`, `pathfinder_map_center`) are not ported (SPEC §8).
- **Tiny potato held items** are kept, returned and dropped, but not drawn on the potato: custom
  blocks can't render item stacks and an entity per held item would not stay in sync with the
  block. **Enchanted names / rune colours** (glint) can't be drawn on a block or on a single
  stack without enchanting it; they still select the variant like Quark. Contributor head equip
  (Quark's patreon reward) is not ported.
- **Tiny potato tilt**: Bedrock block transformations rotate in 90° steps only, so the 2° tilt is
  baked into per-frame geometry (`lepton:jump` frames, 2 ticks each) instead of being continuous.
- **Name sounds**: Quark plays voice clips; Lepton plays original jingles under the same triggers
  (no Quark audio may be copied).
- **Ambience loop** uses a server re-trigger (Bedrock sound definitions can't loop a record).
- **Framed compasses/clocks** (item frames) keep vanilla behaviour: the frame renders through the
  block actor's own sprite calculator.
- **Wrong neighbourhood**: Quark compares the item's `getDisplayName()` (which is `[name]` with
  brackets) with the typo list, so the song never plays in Java; Lepton compares the plain name, as
  intended.
- **Vanilla clients**: compass changes, HUDs and outlines need the client mod (Quark's are
  client-side too); vanilla clients get the action-bar and lore fallbacks above.

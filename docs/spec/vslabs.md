# U1 vslabs: vertical slabs of every vanilla slab material, and slabs back into blocks

Behaviour reference: Quark 4.1-488 (`release-4.1-488+1.21.1`): `content/building/module/VerticalSlabsModule`,
`content/building/block/{VerticalSlabBlock, QuarkVerticalSlabBlock, WeatheringCopperVerticalSlabBlock}`,
`content/tweaks/module/SlabsToBlocksModule`, `content/tweaks/recipe/SlabToBlockRecipe`, the recipes under
`data/quark/recipe/building/{crafting,stonecutting}/vertslabs/`, the loot tables and the tags
`quark:vertical_slab(s)` / `quark:wooden_vertical_slabs`.

## 1. Modules covered

### VerticalSlabs (building, enabled by default)

Adds a vertical slab for every vanilla slab: a half block standing upright against one side of the
block space. Three slabs stacked in a column craft three vertical slabs; one vertical slab crafts back
into one slab. The stone-like ones are also made in the stonecutter (one block -> two vertical slabs,
copper blocks -> eight). Placing a vertical slab puts its full face against the clicked face (clicking
the top or bottom face uses the half of that face the cursor is on). Placing a second vertical slab of
the same kind into the open half merges them into a double vertical slab (a full block that drops
two). Vertical slabs can be waterlogged (not when double). Walls and glass panes / iron bars connect to
the side of a vertical slab (`allowSideConnections`). The copper ones weather like vanilla copper, can
be waxed with honeycomb and scraped / unwaxed with an axe, and are de-oxidized by lightning. Wooden
ones burn in a furnace like their slab (150 ticks) and catch fire like planks. The vertical slabs of
Quark's own materials are added by the units owning those materials (SPEC §2.3); this unit implements
the shared behaviour once, keyed by the `lepton:vertical_slab` tag.

### SlabsToBlocks (tweaks, enabled by default)

Any two identical slabs in a crafting grid (any positions, shapeless) give back the full block the
slab is made from, unless another recipe already matches the grid (e.g. two quartz slabs stacked make
chiseled quartz). The slab -> block table is crawled from every shaped recipe that crafts a slab from
a row of three identical items (vanilla and every mod slab), minus `bannedSlabs`.

### SlabsToBlocks

No blocks or items. Recipes `lepton:slabs_to_blocks_<slab path>` (for non-vanilla slabs
`lepton:slabs_to_blocks_<namespace>_<path>`): shapeless, two of the slab -> one block, created at world
load from the crawl (§3.6).

## 3. Mechanics

### 3.1 Placement and merging (game/Shapes, shared)

Quark `VerticalSlabBlock#getStateForPlacement` / `canBeReplaced`, implemented once in `game/Shapes` for
every `lepton:vertical_slab` block:

- Clicked face horizontal -> `type` = that face. Clicked face up/down -> angle = `atan2(x - 0.5, z - 0.5)`
  of the hit relative to the block, in degrees * -1, `type` = the opposite of the horizontal direction of
  that yaw (the slab ends up on the half of the face you clicked).
- Using the same vertical slab item on a vertical slab: clicking its open face (`face == type`) merges in
  place; clicking a neighbour so the new slab would go into the slab's space merges if the clicked face
  is not the slab's `type`. Result `type=double`, waterlogging removed.

### 3.2 Waterlogging

`minecraft:liquid_detection` lets a non-double vertical slab hold water (placed into a water source,
or water placed into it). The `double` permutation can't hold water, and when a slab becomes double
(merge, setblock, ...) the water in its liquid layer is removed on the next tick
(`BlockChangedEvent`).

### 3.3 Wall / pane / fence connections

Rules (Quark `VerticalSlabsModule#shouldWallConnect` + Java's sturdy-face rule), for a vertical slab of
type `t` seen from a neighbour on its face `f`:

| face | walls, panes, iron bars | fences |
|---|---|---|
| `double` (any face) | connect | connect |
| back face (`f` = opposite of `t`, a full face) | connect | connect |
| side faces (axis differs from `t`) | connect if `enableVerticalSlabs && allowSideConnections` | no |
| open side (`f` = `t`) | no | no |

- Lepton walls/panes/fences (`game/Shapes`): through the `shapes::addConnectRule` extension (contract
  addition, add-only).
- Vanilla walls: hook on `WallBlock::_desiredConnectionState` (server side, wall states are stored);
  a new connection is `tall` when the block above the wall is solid, else `short`.
- Vanilla panes, iron bars and fences are connected by the client renderer from the block's
  `minecraft:connection_rule`: each `type` permutation enables the back face and the two side faces
  (`double`: all four). See §7 for the two consequences.

### 3.4 Copper

`WeatheringCopperVerticalSlabBlock` stages: unaffected -> exposed -> weathered -> oxidized, each with a
waxed twin (`waxed_…`).

- **Weathering** (Java `WeatheringCopper` + `ChangeOverTimeBlock#changeOverTime`): the non-waxed,
  non-oxidized slabs carry `minecraft:tick` with an interval uniform in [1, 8191] ticks (on average one
  tick per 4096 game ticks = one Bedrock random tick at `randomTickSpeed` 1). A native hook on
  `Block::queuedTick` turns each tick into `randomTickSpeed` random-tick attempts (gamerule read live; 0
  disables weathering). An attempt: with chance 0.05688889, count every weathering copper block (vanilla
  copper blocks/slabs/stairs/doors/trapdoors/grates/bulbs/chiseled/... and Lepton's slabs, waxed ones
  excluded) within Manhattan distance 4: if any is younger, nothing happens; else with `k` older and `j`
  same-age ones, `f = (k+1)/(k+j+1)`, chance `f² × (0.75 if unaffected else 1)` to advance one stage. The
  `lepton:type` is kept.
- **Waxing**: using honeycomb on a non-waxed copper slab turns it into the waxed one (honeycomb used up
  unless creative), level event `WaxOn` (particles) + sound `CopperWaxOn`.
- **Axe**: on a waxed slab removes the wax (`WaxOff`, `CopperWaxOff`); on an exposed/weathered/oxidized
  non-waxed slab scrapes one stage back (`Scrape`, sound `Scrape`). The axe takes 1 damage (not in
  creative). Interactions are cancelled and applied on the next tick (AGENTS rule).
- **Lightning** (Java `LightningBolt#clearCopperOnLightningStrike`): when a bolt strikes a lightning rod
  (any copper stage) or a block, the block the rod is attached to (or the struck block) is checked: if it
  is weathering copper, a Lepton slab there becomes unaffected; then 3-5 random walks of 1-8 steps each,
  every step picking the first weathering copper among 10 random positions of the 3x3x3 cube around the
  walk's position, scrape a Lepton slab there one stage (`ParticlesElectricSpark`). Vanilla copper blocks
  are cleared by the game itself.

### 3.5 Fuel and fire

- Wooden vertical slabs (`minecraft:flammable`: catch 5, destroy 20) burn and spread fire like planks.
- Furnace fuel: hook on `FurnaceBlockActor::getItemBurnDuration`: a vertical slab burns as long as its
  slab (Java: 150 ticks, `getBurnTimeInTicksWhenWooden`); vertical slabs of other units' woods burn like
  an oak slab when their block is flammable. Crimson/warped and stone ones are not fuel.

### 3.6 SlabsToBlocks recipes

At world load (`db::onWorldReady`), and again on `/quark reload`, U1 walks the level's recipes
(`Recipes::mRecipes`) like Quark's `ZRecipeCrawl` visitor: a shaped `crafting_table` recipe, 3 wide and 1
high, whose three ingredients are the same item (same aux) and whose result is a slab (vanilla
`isSlabBlock` or a `lepton:slab` block; vertical slabs excluded) gives the entry slab -> ingredient. When
several recipes make one slab, the ingredient whose name is closest to the slab's wins (e.g. purpur
block, not purpur pillar). Each entry becomes a shapeless recipe "2 slabs -> 1 block" loaded through the
game's own JSON recipe loader (`Recipes::_loadRecipe`, `priority` 50 so the vanilla 2-slab recipes such
as chiseled quartz / chiseled stone bricks / chiseled tuff, priority 0, take precedence like Quark's
`checkForOtherRecipes`). Slabs in `bannedSlabs` (item ids) get no recipe.

### 3.7 Disabling (`enableVerticalSlabs` / `enableSlabsToBlocks` = false)

Applied at world load and on `/quark reload` (the recipes are resent to every player with a
`CraftingDataPacket`):

- VerticalSlabs off: every recipe whose result or ingredient is a `lepton:vertical_slab` block (any
  unit's, as Quark registers all vertical slabs in this module) is taken out of the recipe book and the
  crafting/stonecutter matching; placing vertical slabs is refused (`PlayerPlacingBlockEvent`); copper
  weathering/waxing, fuel, side connections and the vanilla wall hook stop.
- SlabsToBlocks off: its recipes are taken out.
- Turning a module back on puts the same recipe objects back.

## 7. Bedrock differences / not ported

- **Textures**: the vertical slabs use the game's vanilla slab textures (no copies, no Quark assets), so
  they always match their slab and follow resource packs. No pixel art of our own is needed.
- **Vanilla panes / iron bars / fences** are connected by the client from `minecraft:connection_rule`,
  which is fixed in the pack: (a) they connect to the sides of vertical slabs whatever
  `allowSideConnections` says (walls, Lepton panes and Lepton fences follow the option), and (b) vanilla
  fences also connect to the sides (one rule per block covers every connection type; Java fences only
  see the full back face). Changing that would need a client-side renderer change for every client,
  vanilla ones included.
- **Random ticks**: custom blocks get no random ticks on Bedrock without scripting; `minecraft:tick` with
  a [1, 8191] interval reproduces the average random tick rate (the distribution is uniform per block
  instead of geometric).
- **Lightning**: vanilla copper next to Lepton slabs is cleared by the game's own random walk, Lepton
  slabs by ours (two independent walks instead of one shared walk).
- **Creative inventory** while VerticalSlabs is off: the creative list is built from the behavior pack
  when the world loads, so the vertical slabs stay listed; they can't be placed and have no recipes.
- **Recipe priority**: vanilla Bedrock resolves a grid that matches two recipes by `priority` (lower
  first). SlabsToBlocks uses 50, vanilla recipes 0, so a vanilla 2-slab recipe wins as in Quark.
- **SlabToBlockRecipe "locked" check** (any other recipe matching the grid wins) is that priority rule.
- **1.26 slabs** (pale oak, resin brick, poplar, cinnabar, sulfur): not in Quark 4.1-488 (MC 1.21.1); added
  under the module's rule "every vanilla slab".
